How it works

Based on College GameDay's weekly Superdog pick. Each week every player picks one underdog.

Cover: your underdog loses by less than the spread → 1 pt.
Outright win: your underdog wins the game → 2 pts.
Losing by exactly the spread is a push (0 pts). Picks lock at kickoff.

The commissioner (the app's owner, and anyone shared as Editor) adds each week's games and enters the final scores. Players need Contributor access to join and pick.
